Arranging duct and vent work before a property is closed for a season

A property closed for part of the year may carry duct, vent or ventilation tasks that need agreeing before the door is locked, not after. The closing date is a fact, so list it for your Muskoka property. Which systems will be shut off or left running and by whom, or what you noticed in the last weeks of use, may be less certain, so list them and say where you are unsure. Also name who will hold the keys and receive the job record while you are away.

  1. Return grilles

    Openings that pull room air back into the system.

  2. Return ducts

    Carry that air back to the furnace.

  3. Furnace blower and coil

    The blower, evaporator coil and cabinet.

  4. Supply trunk

    The largest duct carrying air away from the furnace.

  5. Branch runs

    Smaller ducts feeding each room.

  6. Supply registers

    Where air comes back into each room.

Separate runs to ask about:Dryer ventHRV core and screensBathroom fan ductsRange hood exhaust

  • Closing date and access

    State the date the property closes, who can give the provider access before then, and who holds the keys afterward.

  • Systems left running

    Note which systems stay on or are shut off while the property is empty, and who is responsible for each one.

  • Last weeks of use

    Write down what you noticed in the final weeks, such as odours, weak airflow, lint at an outside hood or fan noise.

Questions About Your Muskoka Request

Would Muskoka Duct Cleaning itself be the one doing the work at my property?

No. Our enquiry team reads each request that comes in and may pass it to an independent provider. Muskoka Duct Cleaning is an enquiry website, not a duct contractor, and nobody here cleans, repairs or visits a property. That provider must give their own name and confirm they can reach your address before any scope, price or visit is discussed.

What happens after I send a request about my Muskoka property?

When you send a request, your description goes to our enquiry team: they review it. It will not secure a date or book a visit, nor will it set a price or confirm a provider. Whoever responds agrees each of those with you separately and in writing, and you may decline at any point before anything is arranged or paid. Nothing is final until you have read the written scope.

How far ahead of closing a Muskoka property should I send a request?

Send it as soon as the closing date is known, since a provider may need time to confirm coverage, access and scope. No timing can be promised, and a provider may not be able to work before you leave. If the date is close, say so plainly and ask whether the work should be scoped now and carried out later.

Who should hold the keys and receive the job record while I am away?

Name a person you rely on to give access, such as a relative, a neighbour or a property manager, and give the provider that contact in writing. The same person, or you by message, should receive photographs and the written record. Do not hand over keys until the provider, scope and pricing are agreed in writing.

Should I send a Muskoka dryer vent request if the dryer will sit unused?

A dryer that will sit unused can still be described in a request. Say what you noticed at the connector and the outside hood in the last weeks, and state that the dryer will not run during the absence. The provider decides whether the vent can be reached and who moves the dryer, and a gas connection is left to appropriately qualified personnel.

Can I close my Muskoka property before a provider confirms the scope?

You may, but nothing can be assumed. Until a provider is identified and has confirmed coverage, scope and pricing in writing, no work is arranged. If you leave first, say so in the request, name who can give access later, and treat any later visit as a separate agreement. A provider may propose a smaller scope or another date.

Who decides whether the HRV or furnace stays on while my Muskoka property is closed?

You do, together with whoever looks after the equipment; this site gives no instructions on shutting down or setting anything. State in your request what will be left running or off and who made that choice. Fuel, combustion, refrigerant, electrical and control questions need appropriately qualified diagnosis rather than a cleaning request, and a gas smell means leaving and contacting the gas supplier or emergency responders.

What should a Muskoka business owner send if the premises close for a season?

Send the lease or ownership position in plain words, the units that serve the space and the name of the landlord or property manager who can approve access. Say which hours are available and whether a kitchen exhaust is involved, since it is a separate system. Do not assume your approval is enough; confirm who may authorize work.
